# Esla — brag plan

**What:** A phone score counter for the road-trip game of spotting Teslas and calling them out by color.
**For:** Families and friends on long drives. Everyone scores from their own phone, live.
**Sets it apart:** A fully codified rulebook with ridiculous names (Wesla, Whuck, Puruck), plus missed-call penalties, gas station ×2 and a +50 dealership.
**Funniest claim:** "White Cybertruck? That's a Whuck." The rulebook is the joke, and it's played completely straight.
**Visual hook:** The made-up call names slamming onto a black screen in their actual swatch colors.
**Real UI:** Play scoreboard cards, ScoreSheet (Tesla/Cybertruck, color grid, ⛽ ×2, Missed, Dealership), toast, flash animation, Results trophy. Built with the app's own styles.css and markup.
**Tone:** default (punchy, playful), with a hint of deadpan sports broadcast.
**Share caption:** Spotting Teslas on road trips now has an official scoring system. Whuck = 3 points.
